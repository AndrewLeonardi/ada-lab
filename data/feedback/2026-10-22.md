# Feedback 2026-10-22 (audit 2)

Auditor: Claude (Opus 5.5) via Claude Code. Audit file: `data/audits/2026-10-22.yaml`.

## Result
- Published accuracy: 16 of 17 randomly sampled verified numbers correct. The one error: "46 in the placebo
  group" was the number assessed for efficacy, not randomized.
- Three sampled numbers came from trials no longer in the public dataset. The sampler now draws only published
  numbers.
- Review queue (random sample) and all 27 consistency contradictions: 11 confirmed, 9 corrected, 41 rejected.

## Patterns found, and the fixes
| Pattern | Examples | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Counts of patients treated, evaluable, assessed or analysed filed as randomized | CAB-ATE 46; ASPECCT 499/500; COIN per-protocol 467/511 | `randomized_count` check: the sentence must tie the count to randomization, with nothing like "assessed" or "received" in between. Prompt v3. |
| The trial total recorded under each arm | TRIBE 508/508; PICCOLO 672/672; CheckMate 8HW 303/303 (9 trials) | Already flagged by `arms_exceed_total`; prompt v3: "never put the trial's total under an arm". |
| Hazard ratio recorded the wrong way round | STRATEGIC-1, TIME, WJOG 6210G, FFCD 2001-02, CUFOX (2), VALENTINO, GISCAD (8) | Already flagged by `hr_direction` (it now ignores medians within 10%); prompt v3 explains the direction rule. |
| Stable disease, early tumour shrinkage or a landmark rate filed as disease control | CO.17, TRIPLETE, COMMIT, afatinib + cetuximab | `dcr_named` check; prompt v3. |
| An 80% interval recorded as 95% | COIN HR 1.084 (80% CI) | `ci_is_95` check; prompt v3. |
| A bound recorded as a value | "<0.5%" recorded as 0.5 | `exact_value` check; prompt v3. |
| Medians in days recorded as weeks | CUFOX: 200 and 502 days | `unit_in_source` check; prompt v3 (days are not recorded). |
| A 10-month PFS rate filed as median PFS | VALENTINO 59.9% / 49.0% | `unit_fits` (already), prompt v3. |

Re-running the free checks on everything stored moved 129 numbers from verified to review: 1,998 became 1,869
before this audit's verdicts, and 1,881 after. Most of these are randomized counts whose sentence does not say
"randomized". Some are surely right (CRYSTAL's "599 received cetuximab" were its randomized arms), but "verified"
should mean provably right.

## Process bugs found and fixed
- The 46 candidate papers awaiting a model check never got one. The free shift only ran when no paid work was
  going on, and was refused the model once a whole batch no longer fitted in the budget. Now one free step runs
  every round, budget-needing checks go first (6 at a time), and they may use what is left of the day's cap.
- Her status flipped between "working" and "free shift" every 45 seconds. Paid screening now keeps her "working".
