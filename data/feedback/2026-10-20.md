## Changes made
1. New measure `other_time_median`, with the endpoint named in `detail` (prompt `extract-v2`).
2. `pfs_median` is now defined in the prompt as time from randomization to the first progression or death.
3. New free code check, `standard_endpoint`. A PFS, OS or TTP median cannot be verified if its quote or its
   own `detail` names a non-standard endpoint. The detail is checked because short quotes such as
   "14.3 months (10.7-20.4)" name no endpoint.
4. `ada reverify` re-runs the checks on stored extractions at no cost.
5. Corrected records are protected from re-extraction. Re-applying an amended audit file no longer logs
   duplicate corrections.

## Watch next time

  queue).
- Response rates measured within one phase of a strategy trial (for example reintroduction ORR in
  OPTIMOX2). They are correct with `detail`, but should not be pooled with first-line ORR.
